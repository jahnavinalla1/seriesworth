## Simulated inputs

Every series-level or deal-level number in `data/` is an invented, deterministic modeling assumption. There are no scraped customer records, private Sony inputs, licensed title datasets or randomly generated records disguised as actuals. "Actual" in a model means the simulated closed-period baseline, not a reported Sony actual.

The Excel Inputs tab separates public context from editable model assumptions. Monetary model outputs are USD millions; yen segment forecasts are context only and are not mixed into model arithmetic. Outcomes are illustrative and must not be presented as business results delivered at Sony. This project is unaffiliated with Sony Pictures Entertainment.
