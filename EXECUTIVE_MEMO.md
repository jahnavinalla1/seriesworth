# Management memo — protect the quality of the ultimate

**Audience:** Scripted TV FP&A and Accounting  
**Status:** Independent fictional case, prepared September 29, 2026  
**Decision:** Escalate the revised assumptions for review and distinguish a planning screen from an accounting conclusion.

Signal House's modeled future sales fall from $52m to $38m after revisions across domestic renewal, international sales and catalog revenue. Lifetime revenue consequently declines from $100m to $86m, while historical results remain unchanged.

The available cost basis is $45m. At the revised revenue outlook, net remaining proceeds in the simplified screen are $44.52m, creating a **$0.48m shortfall**. After the screen and prospective amortization, current contribution becomes a $0.23m loss rather than the previous $2.99m profit. The remaining asset is $30.21m.

The title's $5.52m revised lifetime contribution remains positive. That figure includes historical revenue and costs, and it must not be used to dismiss the shortfall in remaining recovery. Conversely, the screen must not be described as a confirmed $0.48m IFRS impairment: it omits valuation and accounting judgments that require separate review.

A 25% haircut to revised future sales produces a $9.22m screen write-down and an $8.51m current contribution loss. Amortization alone declines in this downside because the screen reduces the amortizable asset first. Management should evaluate **total current charges**, not infer improving economics from lower amortization.

## Proposed review actions

| Action | Proposed owner | Evidence / decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| Challenge each revised revenue window | Distribution / FP&A | Contracts, renewal status, rights availability and comparable sales |
| Separate contracted from uncontracted estimates | Business Affairs / FP&A | Counterparty documentation and explicit assumption ownership |
| Assess accounting implications | Accounting | Applicable recognition, impairment and participation policies; valuation evidence |
| Preserve an approved version of the ultimate | FP&A reviewer | Dated assumptions, variance explanation and reviewer sign-off |

The screen needs approximately **$38.52m of future revenue** to cover the available cost and modeled exploitation/participation deductions. The revised estimate of $38m sits below that threshold. Use the threshold as a review trigger, not as a target to which estimates should be adjusted.

The FP&A analyst improves the process by surfacing deteriorating assumptions promptly, linking them to period reporting, and giving Accounting a traceable set of calculations. No claim of actual Sony savings or prevented misstatement is made.
